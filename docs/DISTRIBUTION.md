# Distribution (Part VIII)

**Read before writing code.** Users come from **findings you can trade**, not announcements.

## Diagnosis

Prior projects failed outreach because they asked for attention with nothing to trade.
Bug reports about **their** artifact get read (~100%). “I built a thing, take a look”
does not.

CP-11 corpus scan + public-VEX audit **mechanically generate** the outreach list with
a per-person hook. That is what the four-week evidence period is for.

## Three structural fixes

1. **Trade findings, never announcements** — open with something true about their
   artifact. No repo link in paragraph one.
2. **Borrow distribution** — merged PR into EMBA, Dependency-Track, Grype, Trivy, or
   OpenSSF toolchain > any follower count. Find the existing issue asking for this.
3. **Enter free rooms** — standards WGs (§7.2) contain compliance buyers who ignore
   cold email.

## Sequencing (non-negotiable)

| Checkpoint | Distribution unlocked |
|------------|----------------------|
| CP-3 | `audit-vex` → collect (not send) public VEX defects |
| CP-4 | Attend standards WGs — listen only |
| CP-9 | Private demo to 1–2 friendly contacts |
| CP-11 | Findings exist → pre-notify named maintainers (**14-day** clock) |
| CP-12 | Launch sequence — [launch/CP-12-SEQUENCE.md](../launch/CP-12-SEQUENCE.md) |
| CP-13 | Integration PRs + Manifest flywheel |

**No outreach before there is a finding to trade.**

## Budget and stop rule

Cap **4 hrs/week** distribution. Channel dead after **20** individually-written messages
with **<3** substantive replies — log date + hook type; do not re-enter with better copy,
re-enter with a better artifact or not at all.

## Skip permanently

Paid ads · Product Hunt · cold enterprise email · broadcast Slack/Discord · personal
brand before artifacts exist.
