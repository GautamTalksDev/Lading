# CP-14 — First revenue conversation (gate)

**Status: CLOSED** — do not execute until the trigger below is satisfied.

This is a **gate**, not a prompt. Do not pitch, cold-call, or force revenue
conversations. CP-12 must produce **unprompted inbound** first.

---

## Prerequisite

CP-12 launch complete (posts published, corpus/manifest licenses live, outreach
sent). If KT-3 fires (no commercial inbound by month 8), CP-14 never opens — stop.

---

## Trigger (all required)

**Three separate companies** have contacted you **unprompted**, each asking for
**one of**:

| Qualifying request |
|------------------|
| Evidence storage / retention |
| CI integration |
| Private Manifest entries for proprietary components |
| Help producing a CRA technical file |

Log each in [INBOUND-LOG.md](INBOUND-LOG.md). Gate opens at **3/3** with dates
and contact names recorded.

---

## Then, and only then

Execute in order. Do not skip steps.

| Step | Action | Artifact |
|------|--------|----------|
| 1 | Talk to **all three**. Ask what audit, customer questionnaire, or regulation created the need; what the deadline is; what they do today instead. **Do not pitch.** Take notes **verbatim**. | [DISCOVERY-NOTES-TEMPLATE.md](DISCOVERY-NOTES-TEMPLATE.md) per company |
| 2 | Offer **consulting first** at **$200–400/hr**: run LADING across their product line; deliver an evidence pack plus an honest gap list. Paid research — learn what the product is before building it. | [CONSULTING-OFFER.md](CONSULTING-OFFER.md) |
| 3 | Build **LADING Evidence** (paid infrastructure) only after **two paid engagements** produced the **same request twice**. | [ENGAGEMENT-LOG.md](ENGAGEMENT-LOG.md) |
| 4 | SaaS pricing: **$600–1,500/mo per org**. Not per seat — infrastructure tied to a **10-year retention obligation**. **Never discount below $600.** | [PAID-TIER-ONE-PAGER.md](PAID-TIER-ONE-PAGER.md) |
| 5 | Before **invoice #1**: register a company, obtain professional indemnity insurance quote, put a **liability cap** in the consulting contract. Regulatory artifacts — not as a private individual. | [ENTITY-CHECKLIST.md](ENTITY-CHECKLIST.md) |

---

## Definition of Done

- [ ] Three unprompted inbound requests documented with **dates and names**
- [ ] ≥1 **paid consulting engagement** completed before any paid **feature** is written
- [ ] Entity registered and insurance **quoted** before invoicing
- [ ] Written one-page paid tier description that a **customer read and agreed to**

Track progress in [INBOUND-LOG.md](INBOUND-LOG.md) and [ENGAGEMENT-LOG.md](ENGAGEMENT-LOG.md).

---

## STOP — paywall line

**Moving a previously-free capability behind a paywall is the fastest way to lose
an open-source audience permanently.**

The free CLI, the free Manifest, and `lading verify` stay **free and fully
featured. Forever.** Nothing that existed at CP-12 ever moves behind a paywall.

Authoritative list: [FREE-FOREVER-BOUNDARY.md](FREE-FOREVER-BOUNDARY.md).

Paid tier adds **new infrastructure** (retention, private manifests, org-scoped
storage) — it does not gate existing CP-12 capabilities.
