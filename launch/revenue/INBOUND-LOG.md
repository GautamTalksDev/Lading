# Inbound log (CP-14 trigger)

**Gate status: CLOSED** (0 / 3 qualifying unprompted contacts)

Only log contacts that **reached out to you** without a sales prompt from you.
Each must ask for one qualifying need (see [CP-14-GATE.md](CP-14-GATE.md)).

Do not count: replies to your CP-12 outreach, conference hallway chats you
initiated, or generic “cool project” mail with no commercial need.
