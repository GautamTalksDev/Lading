## What it is

**LADING Evidence** is org-scoped **infrastructure** for compliance evidence —
not a per-seat scanner license. It sits beside the free CLI:

| Layer | Price | What you get |
|-------|-------|----------------|
| **Free (forever)** | $0 | Full-featured CLI, public Manifest, `lading verify`, GitHub Action (self-hosted) — see [FREE-FOREVER-BOUNDARY.md](FREE-FOREVER-BOUNDARY.md) |
| **LADING Evidence** | **$600–1,500 / month / org** | Retention-backed evidence storage, org policy, optional private manifest entries — **additive only** |

**Not per seat.** Priced for a **10-year retention obligation** on evidence
artifacts tied to regulatory filings.

**Floor:** $600/month/org. No discounts below $600.

---

## What LADING Evidence adds (when built)

Exact scope emerges from paid consulting; typical requests:

- **Evidence retention** — immutable store of bundles + VEX + verification receipts with policy-driven retention (10-year class)
- **CI integration** — org hooks, audit log of `lading verify` outcomes across pipelines (self-hosted Action remains free)
- **Private Manifest** — proprietary component entries not in the public CC0 tree
- **CRA technical file assist** — structured export of Annex I mapping from stored evidence (not legal advice)

Nothing in this tier removes or limits free CLI capabilities.

---

## What stays free forever

The CP-12 feature set: scan, explain, verify, sign, public manifest propose/contribute,
corpus methodology, air-gapped verification. No feature migration to paid.
