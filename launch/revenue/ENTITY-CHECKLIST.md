## Consulting contract essentials

Work with a solicitor for your jurisdiction. Minimum topics:

| Clause | Purpose |
|--------|---------|
| **Liability cap** | Cap fees paid in trailing 12 months or fixed sum — regulatory tooling, not unlimited E&O exposure |
| **Not legal / not notified-body advice** | LADING outputs are technical evidence; customer owns compliance conclusions |
| **No prove-a-negative guarantee** | Tool refuses when evidence insufficient; gaps listed explicitly |
| **IP** | Customer owns their scan outputs; you retain OSS CLI + public Manifest |
| **Data processing** | If they send binaries: retention, deletion, subprocessors |
| **Free tier unchanged** | Contract does not require customer to abandon free CLI |

Store executed template path: `launch/revenue/contracts/` (gitignored if sensitive).
